Pa vs kPa, peak vs RMS

Plane-wave intensity in water is I = p²/(ρc) for RMS pressure (or p²/2ρc for peak amplitude), with ρc ≈ 1.5 × 10⁶ Rayl.

Quoted pressureIf peak amplitudeIf RMSPlausible?
4 Pa≈ 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ W/cm²≈ 1 × 10⁻⁹ W/cm²no below ambient noise; almost certainly a unit typo
4 kPa≈ 0.53 mW/cm²≈ 1.1 mW/cm²yes inside patent's 1–450 mW/cm²
8 kPa≈ 2.1 mW/cm²≈ 4.3 mW/cm²yes
10 kPa≈ 3.3 mW/cm²≈ 6.7 mW/cm²yes
  • Wavelength at 33 kHz in water ≈ 4.5 cm. A beaker or bathtub is only a few wavelengths across, so standing waves and hot/cold spots are the main reproducibility risk. The patent itself mentions sweep and beam steering.
  • Mechanical index ≈ 0.02–0.04, far below diagnostic-imaging limits (1.9). The target is sub-cavitation mechanical stimulation, but at 33 kHz gas nuclei in non-degassed water can cavitate, so degassing is part of the dose.
  • Energy: 30 min at a 50% duty cycle gives ~900 s of sonication, about 0.5–4 J/cm². Heating is negligible, and the bath temperature dominates.

Biological claims

In vitro (one 30-min session)

  • Senescent cells (>90% SA-β-gal+) resume growth: EdU, divisions, population doublings up to passage 24.
  • Reversed: SA-β-gal, p16, p21, cell size, 5mC, H3K9me3, γH2AX, nuclear p53, ROS/mitoROS.
  • Telomere length increases.
  • SASP blocked: IL-6, IL-8, IL-10, IL-15, TNF-α, IFN-γ, VEGF, MIP-1α.
  • RNA-seq: 50 genes up ≥2×, 140 down (incl. IGF2, IGFBP2, FGF7, C1QTNF7).
  • Proposed mechanism: Ca²⁺ entry → autophagy ↑, mTORC1 ↓, SIRT1 moves from nucleus to cytoplasm, mitochondrial fission ↑.

In vivo (C57BL/6J, 22–25 months at start)

  • SA-β-gal area in kidney/pancreas: ~70% (sham) vs 10–20% (LFU); p16/p21 staining down.
  • Wheel running 7–10× sham at 29 and 32 months; treadmill improved in all groups; inverted cling in 3 of 5.
  • Weight and fur maintained, while sham mice lost weight and developed bald spots.
  • Lifespan: D2+D3 combined ≈50% alive at 1000 days; individual groups (n = 7–8) not significant.
  • No tumors at autopsy (small n, very old animals).
  • Diabetes paper: insulin resistance and muscle transcriptome changes reversed in aged mice [S6].

Human trials

NCT07168525 · Healthy aging recruiting

UT Health San Antonio (Barshop), PI Blake Rasmussen, XPRIZE collaborator.

  • n = 20, age ≥ 70, randomized, participant-blinded sham (tub without ultrasound).
  • 45 min × 3/week × 8 weeks in an ultrasound bathtub.
  • Primary: knee-extension strength (Biodex).
  • Secondary: SPPB, TUG, 6MWT, blood senescence (IHC/biochemistry/RNA-seq), immune aging (epigenetics), cognition (face-name memory), DEXA lean and fat mass.
  • Estimated primary completion: Oct 2026.

NCT06562374 · Knee osteoarthritis suspended by IRB

UTMB Galveston, PI Thomas Blackwell.

  • n = 10 (actual), single arm, open label; "non-significant-risk device".
  • 33 kHz, "4 or 10 Pa" (sic), 1.6 s on/off, 30 min, every 2–3 days; transducers potted in epoxy within the bath.
  • Primary: participant-reported safety; secondary: survey-based joint response.
  • Suspended in March 2026. The reason is not public.

Strength of evidence: our reading

  • Single lab and no independent replication. Small mouse groups, and lifespan is not significant per group.
  • The only human safety pilot was suspended by its ethics board. The RCT (n = 20) has not reported.
  • The dose is ambiguous across sources (Pa/kPa, peak/RMS, 1.5/1.6 s, 30/45 min). Nobody outside the group can currently reproduce it. That makes an open, calibrated device a genuine scientific contribution.
  • The in-vitro phenotype is true reversal with proliferation and telomere lengthening, the category with the highest theoretical oncogenic concern. See risks.
