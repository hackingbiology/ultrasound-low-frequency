The single most useful table on this site. For every endpoint used in the original work, it says where it was measured (cells, mice, humans, or only asserted in a patent) and whether a DIY self-experimenter can measure the same thing, with what.

What this table tells us. Three things stand out. First, the mechanism itself (cell-cycle re-entry) is invisible from blood: we can only see its consequences, above all the SASP. Second, the human trial's own primary endpoint is functional, not molecular — muscle strength and walking — so a biohacker measuring grip strength and a 6-minute walk is measuring the same class of thing as UT Health San Antonio. Third, nobody has published the delivered dose for humans, which is the one column where an open project can beat the original.
