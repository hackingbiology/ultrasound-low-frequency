Intellectual property: what is covered, and what a DIY project may do

One patent is granted, and it is about killing tumour cells in the United States. Everything about senescence is still an application, nothing is granted anywhere, and the European cases have a structural problem. Here is the detail, and what follows from it for a non-commercial build in Italy.

Not legal advice. This is a documentary reconstruction from public patent databases, written with AI assistance and not by a qualified patent attorney. Anything involving money, hardware leaving your hands, or another person needs an Italian/EU IP lawyer and, for human use, a medical-device regulatory advisor.

The only granted patent, read closely

US 12,714,885's three independent claims (1 device, 16 method, 38 device/in-vitro) all require the same thing:

an immersion element with a liquid medium + ultrasound transducers generating a sequence of programmed cycles, with modulation, intensity and frequency configured to apply periodic forces that trigger a mechanically-induced apoptotic process in tumour cells, "by periodic stretching of the tumour cells, rather than by heat", without mechanically damaging the cells.

What this means

  • Senescence reversal in normal cells is outside the granted claims. Every independent claim is purpose-limited to tumour cells and apoptosis.
  • Frequency ranges (30–250 kHz, 5–50 kHz), the ≤250 mW/cm² power cap and the ≥2 h duration are dependent claims only. Do not try to design around this patent by choosing a frequency — the independents have no frequency limit at all.
  • Also dependent: robotic arms, phased arrays, machine-learning treatment plans, sensor-driven controllers, sealed transducers, field-shaping vessel geometry.
  • The claims were narrowed during prosecution: the 2022 publication said "target cells", the granted version says "tumour cells". That narrowing is what leaves senescence outside.

The residual argument, US only

  • US apparatus claims are read as capability. The same group's cancer work also used 33 kHz, so an identical rig could be argued to be "configured to" do the tumour thing.
  • Counterweights: the claims require the configuration and programming to be aimed at tumour apoptosis, plus "without mechanically damaging" both populations; claim 1 requires immersing a body part of a subject.
  • Irrelevant in Italy. A US patent covers acts in the United States only (35 USC §271(a)).
  • Practical consequence: keep cancer out of this project's stated purpose, documentation and firmware features. It costs nothing and removes the argument.

Europe: nothing granted, and a structural obstacle

  • The immersion-mechanotherapy family never entered the European phase. Its content — immersion vessel, transducer arrays, programmed cycles, 5–50 / 30–250 kHz, water medium, published 2020 — is therefore free to use in Europe and is prior art.
  • Both senescence families are pending with Italy among the designated states. Neither is granted.
  • EPC Art. 53(c) (and Italian CPI art. 45(4)) excludes methods of therapy from patentability. The senescence claims as filed are therapy methods, so they must be rewritten — as in-vitro claims, device claims, or arguably-cosmetic ones — and will end up materially narrower than the US filing. An ultrasound machine cannot gain novelty from a new therapeutic purpose the way a substance can.
  • Note for the record: the inventors' own bioRxiv preprint (8 Dec 2022) and WO 2023/114011 (published 8 days before the Mechanobiologics PCT filing) are full prior art in Europe against any claim not entitled to the June 2022 priority. Europe has no grace period.
  • The parameter values we care about appear as dependent claims in EP 4 561 696: "32–33 kHz and about 6–7 kPa", 30–45 minute sessions, on/off modulated cycles, beam steering and frequency sweeps to homogenise the field, absorbent material, and literally "full body ultrasound spa".

What this project can do in Italy

Legal basis: CPI art. 68(1)(a) (acts done privately and for non-commercial purposes), art. 68(1)(a-bis) (experimental acts relating to the subject-matter of the patented invention — a broad, purpose-based exemption, much wider than the US equivalent), UPCA art. 27(a)/(b) with the same carve-outs, and territoriality.

✅ Allowed

  • Build prototypes and use them for research and self-experimentation. Nothing is granted in Italy; and if something grants later, the private/experimental exemptions cover exactly this.
  • Run in-vitro experiments, replicate or fail to replicate the published findings, and publish the outcome.
  • Publish the full design: schematics, firmware, BOM, calibration procedure, measured data. Publication is not making, using, offering, selling or importing.
  • Use the published patent documents as a technical source. That is what publication is for.
  • Talk, teach and demo in non-commercial settings.

❌ Not allowed, or not without counsel

  • Selling kits, PCBs, transducer sets or assembled devices — even at cost, even "for research use only". A sale ends the private/non-commercial exemption instantly and triggers EU MDR exposure if a medical purpose is implied.
  • Offering sessions or treatments to other people, paid or free, in any wellness or clinical setting. "Full body ultrasound spa" is literally a pending claim.
  • Making medical claims ("reverses aging", "treats osteoarthritis") anywhere: website, README, talk.
  • Experimenting on third parties without an ethics committee, informed consent and MDR clinical-investigation rules.
  • Crowdfunding a production run — that is an offer for sale.
  • Shipping hardware to the US, which would put you inside §271(a)/(c) if the device reads on the granted claims.

Prior art that protects an open design

  • WO 2020/223359 / WO 2020/223242 (Mechanobiologics' own, 2020): immersion + programmed ultrasound cycles + water + arrays, 5–50 / 30–250 kHz. No European patent from it.
  • KIST US 2021/0244972 (2020): ultrasound device for removing senescent cells, abandoned.
  • Therapeutic low-frequency ultrasound, 20–100 kHz: MIST Therapy (FDA 510(k) K050129, ~40 kHz), Söring Sonoca 25 kHz wound debridement — decades of human exposure at these frequencies.
  • Ultrasonic cleaning baths with sweep and degas modes: commodity hardware that is an immersion vessel with piezo transducers and pulsed drive.
  • The scientific literature itself: the preprint and the Aging Cell paper.

Defensive publication. Everything we design — measured pressures, duty-cycle schedules, tank geometry, absorber design, calibration method — will be published, versioned, tagged and archived with a timestamp (Zenodo DOI plus public git history). That makes it prior art against future filings, including continuations still in prosecution. It protects the commons; it does not grant us freedom to operate against earlier priority dates.

What we monitor, quarterly

  1. US 17/515,025 — whether a continuation was filed before grant (likely; needs USPTO Patent Center).
  2. US 18/217,362 — response to the Feb 2026 non-final rejection.
  3. EP 23850582.0 (EP 4 561 696) — Art. 94(3) communications, amended claims, any intention to grant.
  4. EP 22908221.9 (EP 4 447 814, UT System) — examination after the Apr 2026 supplementary search report. This is the family that most directly claims the senescence protocol in Europe.

If either European case heads toward grant with claims reaching hardware or in-vitro use, this analysis gets re-run before anything changes. Third-party observations under EPC Art. 115 are free and anonymous, and the prior art above is the ammunition.
