**IP conclusions (research note 03):** only GRANTED patent is US 12,714,885 B2 (25 Aug 2026, Mechanobiologics) — all independent claims limited to TUMOR cells + mechanically-induced apoptosis; US-only, that family never entered the European phase. Senescence families (US 18/217,362 + EP 4 561 696; UT System US 2024/0399175 + EP 4 447 814, Italy designated) are all PENDING; EPC Art. 53(c) blocks the therapy-method claims. Italy: CPI art. 68(1)(a)/(a-bis) + UPCA 27 → private non-commercial experimental build + publication is fine; NEVER sell kits, treat third parties or make medical claims.

**Biomarker conclusions (note 04):** no gold standard; **GDF-15 = only credible n-of-1 primary endpoint** (RCV ±21.6% single, ±12.5% with 3+3 samples); IL-6 ±62%, hs-CRP ±120% → useless alone. Two LFU-specific traps: LIPUS acutely INCREASES SASP, and T-cell p16 is induced by Ca²⁺ activation (LFU works via Piezo1/Ca²⁺) → draw blood ≥48–72 h after a session. Epigenetic clocks unusable at 8 weeks (biological ICC 0.4–0.7). Protocol: 3 baselines (−4,−2,0), 8 weeks 45 min ×3/wk, 3 post (8,9,10), washout re-test week 16. Min panel ≈ €900–2,400.

**Hardware conclusions (note 05, primary sources recovered 2026-09-16):** the paper's "ONDA MCT-2000" is a **cavitation meter**, not a needle hydrophone (time-averaged total pressure; kPa scale needs a self-calibration the paper never mentions; their field map is in mV with 9× spread). Supplementary Fig S1A = the TimePie slide (135–275 V drive amplitude, 32,248 Hz, 600 pulses, 7–10 cm, "4–8 Pa"). Sister papers name the hardware: Beijing Ultrasonic PZT4 rings + aluminium cone (cancer rig) and **APC 90-4050 Langevin + PiezoDrive PDU 210 + hydrophone→Digilent scope (IISc Bangalore rig = directly copyable)**. **A stock 33 kHz Langevin costs $8.50 (BJC-3360T-48HS, 48 mm face → cone probably unnecessary).** Power needed: 0.1–3 W electrical for a 4 L beaker, 2–150 W for a tub → **SELV feasible**. Commercial cleaning baths CANNOT be turned down (need −12…−27 dB, best dial gives −10 dB). Costed builds: €150 budget / **€640 recommended (SDG1032X + PiezoDrive PDm200)** / €4,500 reference-identical; tub €2,800–15,000 where metrology + electrical safety dominate. Hydrophone rule: buy PASSIVE (Aquarian AS-1 $413), preamplified models clip; medical needle hydrophones invalid at 33 kHz; only NPL(UK) and VNIIFTRI hold a 32 kHz calibration. AJP-Endo diabetes paper is **NEGATIVE** (no glucose/function improvement).

**Acoustic + electrical safety (note 06):** MI is meaningless below ~500 kHz. Inertial cavitation threshold ~130 kPa (13–32× margin) BUT rectified-diffusion/stable cavitation of ~100 µm resonant bubbles has a threshold of only a few kPa → degassing is the whole safety case. **ACGIH waterborne TLV = 177 dB re 1 µPa ≈ 1.0 kPa peak → the 4–8 kPa dose is 12–18 dB ABOVE the only applicable human limit; the live risk is TINNITUS, not tissue damage** (head out of water, hearing protection, baseline audiogram). **No RCD protects a bather** (let-go ~5 mA in water; no RCD type characterised above 1 kHz) → floating supply + 2×MOPP + **SELV along the cable with the step-up transformer inside the potted head** + insulation monitoring + neutral-generator topology (net-zero feed voltage, from WO2020223359). No product standard covers this device.

**Home bathtub direction (Fabio, 2026-09-17):** goal is to use the bathtub already at home, not a purpose-built tub. Page `home-bathtub.html` (nav "Home bathtub"): battery-powered floating 12 V LiFePO₄ retrofit kit, 4–8 potted 33 kHz heads with step-up sealed inside, driver box outside bathroom zone 2 (CEI 64-8 sez. 701: tub interior = zone 0, 12 V a.c./30 V d.c.), sentinel hydrophone every session, coarse jig mapping per tub, fill hot + cool covered (no real degassing), sit upright ears out, kit removed after each session; acrylic tub best, enamelled steel/cast iron reverberant and possibly earth-bonded. Staged path: bench → foot bath (15–25 L, degassable) → tub lower body → mid-chest; never head immersion. Kit ≈ €1,200–2,000 incl. measurement gear.

**Cancer branch / mechanoptosis (added 2026-10-12, page `cancer-branch.html`):** Tijore et al. BTM 2025 (PMC11883105) = same lab, same rig (PZT4 rings + Al cone, Arduino-generated signals, degassed water, sample 8 cm up), 33 kHz, 2 h, 50% duty 1s/1s, **39 mW/cm² ≈ 34 kPa peak** → apoptosis 46% MDA-MB-231 / 58% A375p / 32% HT1080 vs 9/4/5% controls; matched normal lines unaffected (MCF10A 16 vs 15%, HFF 3 vs 4%); mechanism Piezo1→Ca²⁺→calpain→mitochondrial apoptosis, selectivity because tumour lines lack rigidity sensors (Tpm2.1, myosin IIA). **Two results nobody quotes: in mice (39 kHz, 1 h/day) 400 V/166 mW/cm² gave MORE tumour growth (~10 mm²) than untreated controls (4–5 mm²) — authors say the killing window is "relatively narrow"; chick-embryo survival fell to 60% after the 2nd round.** Dose ladder (peak kPa at ~33 kHz): 2–5 growth, **4–8 rejuvenation**, 10–20 kills normal stem cells, ~34 tumour apoptosis, ~39 best in mice, ~70 worse than control, 80–130 cleaning bath/cavitation. **Rejuvenation vs apoptosis = only 12–18 dB apart.** Decisions: project does NOT build a cancer device (also the only granted patent US 12,714,885 covers exactly tumour apoptosis); cancer assay used only as **biological dosimeter** (34 kPa kills tumour lines, normal lines as negative control) + sharper oncogenic-safety arm (counter-evidence: Nat Commun 2025 PMID 40890143, capillary constrictions prime tumorigenicity via PIEZO1). New questions A12, E10, E11.

**HAND-FIRST è il percorso di test deciso (Fabio, 2026-10-14).** Pagina `hand-first.html` (nav "Hand first"), prima tappa prima della vasca. Bersaglio = **mano** (non piede); misure **solo non invasive per ora** (biopsie appaiate p16/p21/SA-β-gal deliberatamente rimandate). Il punto chiave non è la sicurezza ma il **controllo controlaterale** + vera cecità (LFU impercettibile → due bacinelle identiche, allocazione sorteggiata da terzi): n-of-1 randomizzato, sham-controllato, in crossover — disegno impossibile con la vasca intera. Endpoint primario candidato: **velocità di crescita dell'unghia** (segno alla lunula + calibro, ~3 mm/mese); poi foto standardizzate, forza di presa/pinza, termocamera, idratazione/TEWL, spessore dermico (eco cutanea), vibrazione/due punti come sicurezza. Il sangue NON è l'endpoint (massa trattata trascurabile). Precedenti a supporto: D+Q ha misurato p16/p21 nell'epidermide (−38%/−30%); Mechanobiologics rivendica proprio spessore cutaneo ed elasticità. Rig: 2 bacinelle 5–15 L (unico volume davvero degasabile, DO ≤2 mg/L), jig fisso 7–10 cm, batteria flottante + SELV, idrofono sentinella. Protocollo: 3 baseline (−4,−2,0) → 8 sett. 3×/sett. → washout → crossover → settimana 21 unblinding e pubblicazione anche se nulla.
